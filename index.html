BUILT ON THE REAL CITY

Helsinki, from Helsinki.

Streets follow surveyed polygons. Most buildings retain municipal 3D geometry and photographic façade textures; the cathedral, its pavilions, Government Palace frontage and five key Kauppatori building frontages have photo-guided geometric reconstructions. Kauppatori uses mapped paving and photo-positioned market canopies. Trams follow a dated HSL route snapshot, including shared streets and crossings.

A prototype, not a survey-exact digital twin. Street terrain is flattened; landmark stair heights, ornaments, landscape inference and some façades remain approximate. Market monuments are simplified silhouettes; rear façades and some source texture gaps remain. Tram rails follow route shapes, not surveyed rail centres. Switches, overhead wires, stop matching, traffic priorities and timings are simplified; this is not live HSL tracking.
